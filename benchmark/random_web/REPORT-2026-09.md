## Reading it

Columns: *missing* = the page's words we did not produce (recall); *extra* = our words the
page does not show.

- The curated suites (fidelity, live) sit at recall ≈ 0.99; the random web costs about
  two points at the median (0.986) and shows a real tail: 27 of 258 scored pages under
  0.80 recall.
- Refusals are honest and counted apart from wrong output: 16 of 300, ten of them pages
  that render empty.
- `extra > 0.30` on 53 pages is the larger open problem. Where the extra words are consent
  and personal-data text (beonemedicines.com: "cookies" ×22; epldiamond.ru: "персональных
  данных" ×136) it is the hidden-but-openable dialog case the owner chose to leave for now.
- **gokitetours.com** (1,021 page words, recall 0, extra 0) is the one row that breaks the
  product rule: the engine produced no words and did not refuse. First item on the list.
- Two rows are the scorer's own gaps, not the engine's: nsenergybusiness.com (recall 0,
  extra 1.0) is Chromium receiving a 15-word 403 page while the engine read the article --
  an oracle-side block `report.py` files under *scored*; flickfusion.net and lumiradx.com
  (recall 1.0, extra 1.0) are pages whose visible text is empty, so a two-word output
  scores as all-extra. `report.py` should file an HTTP-error oracle under *oracle blocked*
  and give a zero-word non-refusal its own bucket; neither is done in this PR.
- The rest of the recall floor -- a login page (xchat.cz), a live-stream page
  (olympicweb.me, 2,893 words, recall 0.03), a Bengali quiz (quizzory.in) -- is undiagnosed;
  open each with `tools/inspect_page.py` before choosing a fix.

Re-score the identical seed after any change to blocks, the union, ordering or rendering:
`make bench-random-web`, then diff the bands against this file.
