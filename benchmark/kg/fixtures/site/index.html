Sode Valley Institute of Technology

Sode Valley Institute of Technology (SVIT) is an engineering college in Udupi, Karnataka, founded in 1998 by the Sode Valley Education Trust. The institute is affiliated with Visvesvaraya Technological University and approved by the All India Council for Technical Education.

About the institute

SVIT runs four undergraduate programmes and two postgraduate programmes across its departments. The campus sits on 42 acres beside the Swarna river and houses 1,800 students. The Department of Computer Science and Engineering is the largest department, with 24 faculty members.

The principal of Sode Valley Institute of Technology is Dr. Meera Nayak, who joined the institute in 2015 after fifteen years at the National Institute of Technology Karnataka.
