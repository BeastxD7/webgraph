Programmes offered

Sode Valley Institute of Technology offers the following programmes. Admission to every undergraduate programme is through the Karnataka Common Entrance Test.

B.E. Computer Science

B.E. Computer Science is a four-year undergraduate programme run by the Department of Computer Science and Engineering. The programme admits 120 students a year and is accredited by the National Board of Accreditation until June 2028.

The programme is headed by Dr. Anita Rao, who is the Head of the Department of Computer Science and Engineering. Core subjects include data structures, operating systems, computer networks and machine learning.

M.Tech Data Science

M.Tech Data Science is a two-year postgraduate programme with an intake of 18 students. The programme coordinator is Prof. Kiran Shetty, who teaches the statistical learning and big data systems courses.

Graduates of M.Tech Data Science have joined Infosys, Wipro and Robert Bosch Engineering as data engineers.

B.E. Electronics and Communication

B.E. Electronics and Communication is a four-year programme with an intake of 60 students, run by the Department of Electronics and Communication Engineering, headed by Dr. Suresh Bhat.
