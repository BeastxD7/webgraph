Events

TechFest 2026

TechFest 2026 is the annual technical festival of Sode Valley Institute of Technology. TechFest 2026 starts on 3 October 2026 and runs until 5 October 2026 in the Main Auditorium. The festival is organised by the Department of Computer Science and Engineering and is convened by Dr. Anita Rao.

Registration for TechFest 2026 costs ₹250 per participant and closes on 25 September 2026. The keynote will be delivered by Dr. Meera Nayak, Principal of the institute.

Alumni meet

The annual alumni meet of Sode Valley Institute of Technology takes place on 20 December 2026 at the Main Auditorium. Alumni of B.E. Computer Science from the batches of 2002 to 2012 are invited.
