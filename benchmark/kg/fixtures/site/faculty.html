Faculty

Dr. Anita Rao

Dr. Anita Rao is the Head of the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at Sode Valley Institute of Technology. She holds a PhD from the Indian Institute of Science and joined SVIT in 2009. Her research is in distributed systems, and she teaches operating systems to the B.E. Computer Science second year.

Dr. Anita Rao can be reached at anita.rao@svit.example.

Prof. Kiran Shetty

Prof. Kiran Shetty is the programme coordinator of M.Tech Data Science and teaches statistical learning. He was previously a data scientist at Robert Bosch Engineering in Bengaluru for six years.

Dr. Suresh Bhat

Dr. Suresh Bhat heads the Department of Electronics and Communication Engineering and teaches signal processing. He is a senior member of the Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers.

Dr. Meera Nayak

Dr. Meera Nayak is the Principal of Sode Valley Institute of Technology. Before SVIT she spent fifteen years at the National Institute of Technology Karnataka, where she was professor of mechanical engineering.
