Fee structure for 2026-27

Fees at Sode Valley Institute of Technology are fixed by the Fee Regulatory Committee of Karnataka and are payable in two instalments, in July and in January.

Tuition fees

The tuition fee for B.E. Computer Science is ₹1,20,000 per year for students admitted through the Karnataka Common Entrance Test. The tuition fee for B.E. Electronics and Communication is ₹98,000 per year.

The tuition fee for M.Tech Data Science is ₹95,000 per year, and GATE-qualified students receive a stipend of ₹12,400 per month from the All India Council for Technical Education.

Hostel and other charges

The hostel fee is ₹45,000 per year for a shared room and ₹68,000 per year for a single room, inclusive of mess charges. A one-time caution deposit of ₹10,000 is refundable when the student leaves.
