# Creative and award-winning sites -- the adversarial corpus.
#
# Chosen because they break the assumptions every extractor makes. These are not sites with
# articles on them; they are sites built to be experienced, where the text is frequently in a
# canvas, split into per-character spans for animation, mounted on scroll, or absent from the
# DOM until a WebGL scene finishes loading.
#
# The measurement that matters here is not F1 against a ground truth -- there is none. It is
# **recovery**: engine-extracted characters over the browser's own `document.body.innerText`.
# A recovery near 1.0 means the engine saw what a reader sees. A low one means content is
# somewhere the DOM cannot reach.
